First Time Ever – One Day Only

For the first time in our history, Tustain Carriers are opening the gates to our yard for a very special, one-day event. Over the past 12 months, we’ve carried out house clearances across the region, uncovering an incredible mix of antiques, vintage treasures, and unique modern finds along the way. Usually, these pieces are sold quietly behind the scenes – but this time, we’re bringing everything together and inviting you in.

From characterful furniture and decorative pieces to quirky curiosities and quality everyday items, there’s something for collectors, interior lovers, resellers, and bargain hunters alike. Every item has a story – and once it’s gone, it’s gone.

This is a genuine, one-off opportunity to explore the hidden side of Tustain Carriers and discover pieces you simply won’t find anywhere else. One yard. One day. One chance. A real event not to be missed.
